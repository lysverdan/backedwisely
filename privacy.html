Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

The short version: this is a simple website, and it collects almost nothing. No accounts, no sign-up forms, no advertising trackers. Here are the details.

Who we are

This site, backedwisely.com, is operated by Backed Wisely Inc., a US-based company. We build independent tools that help people make confident decisions, backed by verified data.

What we collect

We do not ask you for any personal information on this site. There are no accounts, no login, and no forms that collect your name, email, or payment details.

We use Plausible Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how many people visit and which pages they read. Plausible is a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not use cookies and does not collect or store any personal or identifiable information. It does not track you across sites or over time. You can read how it works at plausible.io/data-policy.

As with any website, our hosting provider may process basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) as part of delivering the page to you. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages; see GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies of its own, and our analytics does not use cookies either.

Third parties

We load fonts from Google Fonts and analytics from Plausible. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with anyone — because we do not collect it in the first place. Links to other sites, including our product Yes Car No Car, are governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email us at hello@backedwisely.com.
